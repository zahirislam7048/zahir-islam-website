What is local SEO?

Being the business people find when they search nearby

When someone searches "restaurant near me" or "tuition centre in my area", Google shows a map with three businesses, then a list of websites. Local SEO is the work that gets your business into those spots and makes people choose you once they see you.

It covers your Google Business Profile, your website, your reviews and how consistently your business details appear across the web. When all of these work together, Google trusts your business and shows it to more people nearby.

The problem

Why good businesses stay invisible in local search

01

An incomplete profile

Missing categories, hours, photos or services tell Google your business is less relevant than competitors.

02

Few or old reviews

Customers compare star ratings and recent reviews. A quiet profile loses the click to a busier one.

03

A website Google can't connect

If your website doesn't clearly say what you do and where, Google can't match it to local searches.

FAQ

Local SEO questions, answered directly

How long does local SEO take to work?

Quick fixes to your profile can show results within weeks. Bigger gains usually build over 3 to 6 months, depending on how competitive your area is.

Do I need a website for local SEO?

Your Google Business Profile can bring calls on its own, but a good website makes your profile stronger and helps you rank for more searches.

Can you guarantee the #1 spot on Google Maps?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking, because Google decides. I can guarantee the work, the reporting and a clear plan based on what has worked for my clients.
