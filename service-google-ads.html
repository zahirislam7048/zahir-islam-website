The problem

Stop wasting your Google Ads budget

Most businesses that try Google Ads lose money in the same few places: broad keywords that attract the wrong people, no conversion tracking, and landing pages that don't turn visitors into enquiries.

The result is plenty of clicks and very few customers. The problem is rarely Google Ads itself. It's how the account is set up and managed.

  • No conversion tracking, so nobody knows what works
  • Broad keywords paying for irrelevant searches
  • No negative keywords
  • Ads sending people to the home page
  • "Set and forget" campaigns that are never improved
My system

Search → Click → Landing page → Lead → Customer

I manage the whole path, not just the ads. Each step is tracked so we can see exactly where money turns into customers and where it leaks.

  • Conversion and call tracking setup
