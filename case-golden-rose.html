The client

A cosmetics brand that shoppers search for by name

Golden Rose is a cosmetics brand sold in Bangladesh through Anayase.com. Shoppers search for specific products and prices, like "golden rose lipstick price in bangladesh", so the brand's page needed to show up for those searches.

The goal was to rank the Golden Rose page at the top of Google for brand and product searches.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Brand page SEOOptimised the Golden Rose page on Anayase.com for the brand name and its products.
  2. 02
    Price searchesTargeted "price in bangladesh" searches for foundation, lipstick and face powder, where shoppers are ready to buy.
  3. 03
    Product-level keywordsCovered each product type so the page appears for many different Golden Rose searches.
Results · Google Search Console

6,930 clicks to the Golden Rose page

Over 16 months the page earned 6,930 clicks and 304,000 impressions with an average position of 6.5. Traffic stayed steady throughout, at around 10 to 30 clicks a day.

Keyword rankings

Top positions for Golden Rose searches

Anayase.com ranks at position 1.1 for "golden rose foundation price in bangladesh" and 1.2 for "golden rose lipstick price in bangladesh" and "golden rose face powder price in bangladesh".
