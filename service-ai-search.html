What is AI search optimization?

SEO for the answers, not just the links

Traditional SEO gets your website into Google's list of links. AI search optimization (also called LLM SEO or GEO) gets your business into the answer itself, when someone asks an AI tool a question like "best tuition centre near me" or "where to buy a treadmill in Dhaka".

AI tools build their answers from websites, reviews, business profiles and other sources they trust. The work is making sure your business is clearly described, well reviewed and consistently mentioned in those places.

How it fits

AI search doesn't replace SEO. It builds on it.

Traditional SEO

Ranks your pages in Google's results so people click through to your site.

AI search optimization

Makes your business the one AI tools name, quote and link to when they answer a question.

A strong SEO foundation is what AI tools read first, so the two work best together.

FAQ

Questions about AI search optimization

What is LLM SEO?

LLM SEO means optimising your business so large language models (the AI behind ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI features) understand it and mention it in their answers.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. Nobody controls what AI tools say. I improve the signals they rely on and measure the results honestly every month.

Do I still need regular SEO?

Yes. AI tools lean heavily on websites that already rank well and are trusted, so SEO is the foundation.

How long does it take?

Some changes, like fixing your business details and adding clear FAQs, can show up within weeks. Building mentions and authority takes a few months.

How do you measure AI visibility?

I test real customer questions across AI tools each month and use tools like Semrush to track mentions and cited pages.
