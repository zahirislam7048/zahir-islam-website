The client

A fitness store competing for high-value product searches

Fitnessmart.com.bd sells treadmills, dumbbells, gym equipment and indoor sports gear like carrom boards and punching bags across Bangladesh. Shoppers usually start by searching for a product and its price, so ranking for those searches is where the sales come from.

The goal was to rank category pages at the top of Google for "price in Bangladesh" searches.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Category pages for buying searchesOptimised category pages like treadmill, dumbbell, carrom board and boxing for "price in Bangladesh" searches.
  2. 02
    Link buildingBuilt authority with links from 482 referring domains.
  3. 03
    Big head termsTargeted large searches like "carrom board" (165K searches a month), where the site appears in Google's AI Overview.
Results · Google Search Console

96,200 clicks and 1.39 million impressions in 6 months

From late September 2025 to March 2026 the site earned 96,200 clicks from Google Search with an average position of 5.7 and a strong 6.9% click-through rate. The top search, "treadmill price in bangladesh", alone brought 11,329 clicks.
