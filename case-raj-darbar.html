The client

A highway restaurant that lives on travellers finding it

Raj Darbar Highway Restaurant is open 24 hours on the highway at Risatola. Most of its customers are travellers who search Google Maps for somewhere to eat on the road, so showing up on Maps with the right information is how it gets new customers.

The goal was to get more travellers to find, call and visit the restaurant from Google.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Completed the profileAdded opening hours, phone number, service options, price range and a menu link, so the profile is marked "Looks good".
  2. 02
    Photos and menuAdded photos of the restaurant and a menu, so travellers can see what they get before they stop.
  3. 03
    ReviewsEncouraged guests to leave reviews. The profile now has 272 Google reviews.
