The client

A property company that needed serious buyers, not random clicks

Dreamway Holdings Ltd develops and sells property. Buying property is a big decision, so the company needed to reach a large audience cheaply and turn interested people into real enquiries.

The goal was to build awareness and generate conversions on a small budget.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Lead forms on MetaFacebook and Instagram lead form ads collected buyer details in a couple of taps, and budget moved to the ad set with the lowest cost per lead.
  2. 02
    Chose Demand Gen on GoogleGoogle's Demand Gen campaigns show visual ads across YouTube, Discover and Gmail, where people browse before they search.
  3. 03
    Kept costs lowThe Google campaign delivered 15,800 clicks at under one cent each.
  4. 04
    Tracked conversionsConversion tracking showed which clicks turned into real actions, so the budget went where it worked.
Meta Ads · Full account

17,458 leads across 105 ad sets

I ran lead, messaging, traffic, video, engagement, page-follow and reach campaigns for Dreamway Holdings on Facebook and Instagram. Lead ad sets averaged $2.27 per lead, and messaging ads started 3,584 conversations with interested buyers at about $0.37 each. The biggest lead ad set alone brought 3,981 leads at $1.99 each.

SEO · Google Search Console

From almost no search traffic to 60+ clicks a day

In May 2026 the website got only a handful of visitors from Google each day. After the SEO work, daily clicks climbed steadily from July and reached 60 to 90 clicks a day by September. Over 6 months the site earned 4,810 clicks and 207,000 impressions, with an average position of 6.2, on the first page of Google.
