FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long before I see results?

Many profiles see more views and calls within the first few weeks after optimisation. Ranking higher in the map pack in a competitive area usually takes a few months of steady work.

Do I need to give you access to my profile?

Yes, as a manager. You stay the owner, and you can remove my access at any time.

Can you get rid of bad reviews?

I can't delete honest reviews, but I reply to them professionally and report reviews that break Google's rules. More good reviews over time matter more than one bad one.

What if my profile is suspended?

I can help you understand why and prepare the reinstatement request with the right documents.
