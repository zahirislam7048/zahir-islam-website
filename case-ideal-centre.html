The client

A local tuition centre that needed parents to find them

Ideal Centre for Tuition teaches Maths, English and Science to school students in London. Parents choose a tutor carefully, so the centre needed to reach families nearby and give them an easy way to ask questions before enrolling.

The goal for this campaign was simple: get more enquiries from local parents ahead of the new school term.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Timed it for back-to-schoolLaunched in mid-August, when parents start planning tuition for the new term.
  2. 02
    Used Meta lead formsParents could send an enquiry inside Facebook and Instagram without leaving the app.
  3. 03
    Targeted local familiesAds reached parents within travelling distance of the centre.
  4. 04
    Monitored and adjustedChecked results daily and edited the campaign when lead flow dipped.
