The client

A 24-hour restaurant in one of Dubai's busiest areas

Muhammad Iqbal Restaurant serves Pakistani and Indian food around the clock at Clover Bay Tower in Business Bay, Dubai. The area is packed with restaurants, so the challenge is to be the one people pick when they search "restaurants" or "pakistani restaurant near me" on Google Maps.

The goal was to win local searches and turn them into calls, orders and visits.

What I did

The approach

  1. 01
    Optimised for local searchesSet up the profile to rank for searches like "restaurants", "pakistani restaurant near me" and "pakistani restaurant".
  2. 02
    Photos, video and menuAdded food photos, a video and the menu, so hungry people can decide quickly.
  3. 03
    Reviews and trustBuilt up 461 Google reviews with a 4.6-star average.
Visibility

222,000 people saw the profile

Most people found the restaurant on Google Maps on their phone (68%), which is exactly where hungry people nearby are looking. The profile showed up in 105,000 searches.

Results

20,108 interactions, 4,632 calls and 560 chats

Between May and October 2026 the profile brought 20,108 customer interactions, including 4,632 phone calls (more than 750 in each full month) and 560 chat messages. October is a partial month in every chart.
