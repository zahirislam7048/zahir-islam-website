Local SEO Tool · Free account

See where you rank on Google Maps, street by street

Your Google Maps ranking changes depending on where the customer is standing. The Local SEO Tool checks your ranking for a keyword at every point of a grid around your business, so you can see exactly where you show up and where competitors win.

Step 1

Sign up free

Create an account with your name and email.

Step 2

Connect your business

Search your business name and pick it from Google Maps.

Step 3

Choose a keyword and grid

For example "restaurant near me", a 9 × 9 grid with points 2 miles apart.

Step 4

See your ranking map

Every point shows your rank, colour-coded, with average rank and the share of points where you're in the top 3.

  • Grid sizes 3 × 3 up to 9 × 9
  • Point spacing from 0.5 to 3 miles
  • Rank at every point, plus who ranks top 3 there
  • Scan history for each business and keyword
  • 5 free scans every month
  • Up to 3 businesses per account
